About Me

My roots, interests, and the experiences shaping my journey.

I was born in San Jose del Monte City, Bulacan, and I currently live in Victoria, Oriental Mindoro. Growing up and experiencing different places has helped shape my perspective and appreciation for learning.

I am an aspiring Information Technology professional with a passion for technology, programming, and digital creativity. I enjoy exploring how computers and software work, learning new tools, and creating things that can be useful to others.

I am patient, willing to learn, and adaptable. I believe that improvement comes from practice, curiosity, and being open to challenges. Outside academics, I enjoy content creation and running a small business, which have helped me develop initiative and practical responsibility.

As an SK Chairperson, I also carry responsibilities in my community. I aim to keep growing—not only in technical knowledge, but also as a person who can contribute and lead with purpose.
